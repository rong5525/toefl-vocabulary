Additional vocabulary sources
=============================

School curriculum lists
-----------------------
Primary and junior-high stage divisions follow the public vocabulary appendix
of China's Compulsory Education English Curriculum Standard (2022 edition).
The senior-high additions are derived from a public transcription of the 2025
general senior-high vocabulary list, with compulsory-stage entries removed.

New General Service List 1.2
----------------------------
https://www.newgeneralservicelist.com/new-general-service-list
Used under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC BY-SA 4.0).

150 Most Common Phrasal Verbs
-----------------------------
https://ccaps.umn.edu/esl-resources/students/vocabulary/150-most-common-phrasal-verbs
Definitions and ordering are sourced from University of Minnesota CCAPS.

Most Common American Idioms
---------------------------
https://github.com/xiaolai/most-common-american-idioms
The upstream author states "Copyright NOT reserved". This application uses a
cleaned subset: exact duplicates and entries missing a Chinese explanation,
English explanation, or example sentence are removed. Upstream content was
largely AI-generated and may still contain occasional inaccuracies.
